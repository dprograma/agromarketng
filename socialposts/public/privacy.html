🌾 Privacy Policy

AgroMarket Social Posting Tool  ·  Last updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the AgroMarket Social Posting Tool ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information. The App is operated by AgroMarket Nigeria to automate social media content for agromarketng.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • OAuth access tokens — required to post to your connected social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok)
  • Social account identifiers — your Page ID, Person URN, or Open ID to identify where to post
  • Generated post content — stored temporarily in a local database for review and scheduling
  • Activity logs — records of posts published, failed, or scheduled for monitoring purposes

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and publish content to your connected social media accounts
  • Schedule and track automated posts
  • Display publishing history in the dashboard

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third parties.

4. Data from Social Media Platforms

When you connect a social media account, we receive and store only the minimum data required to publish content:

  • Facebook / Instagram: Page Access Token, Page ID, Instagram Business Account ID
  • LinkedIn: Access Token, Person URN
  • TikTok: Access Token, Open ID

We do not access your followers, messages, personal profile data, or any other data beyond what is needed to publish posts.

5. Data Storage

Data is stored in a SQLite database on the hosting server (Render). Access tokens stored as environment variables are encrypted at rest by the hosting provider. No personal data is stored in external databases or shared with third parties.

6. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are retained until you disconnect the account from the App or revoke access from the respective social media platform. Generated post content is retained in the local database for up to 90 days.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Disconnect any social media account from the App's Settings page
  • Revoke the App's permissions directly from Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, or TikTok account settings
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us

8. TikTok Data

This App uses the TikTok API to publish video content. By connecting your TikTok account, you agree to TikTok's Terms of Service and TikTok's Privacy Policy. TikTok data obtained through the API is used only to publish content and is not shared with any third parties.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data, including secure HTTPS connections and environment-level encryption for sensitive credentials. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@agromarketng.com or visit agromarketng.com.
